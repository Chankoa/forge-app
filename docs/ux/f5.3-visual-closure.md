# F5.3 Visual Closure

## Architecture

F5.3 keeps two deliberate shells:

- Workspace shell: `/app`, `/app/courses`, `/app/explore`, `/app/create` use the collapsible, icon-capable sidebar.
- Course shell: course Overview, Learn, Edit, and Publication omit the Workspace sidebar and use the dedicated course top bar, course context, Structure, content, and Forge composition.

## Presentation

- `CourseCard` shares `CourseCover`, `RelationPills`, `ProgressRing`, and `CourseMeta` between Mes parcours and Explorer.
- The relationship mapping is ordered and truthful: learner only, owner only, and learner plus owner are covered in `course-presentation.test.ts`.
- The SVG ring uses the actual rounded percentage, exposes `aria-label="Progression : n %"`, and disables its fill animation for reduced motion.
- Structure uses empty, current, and checked timeline markers with a connecting line; labels additionally express the state.
- Forge retains default, expanded, and collapsed desktop states. Structure and Forge collapsed rails retain their identity icon and a separate reopen control.

## Responsive And Functional Checks

- 1440px: Workspace, Mes parcours, Course Overview, Learn, Edit, Publication, and dark theme rendered without horizontal overflow.
- 900px and 390px: Structure and Forge compact panels open; their `Escape` handlers close dialogs through bubbling keyboard events; no horizontal overflow observed.
- 390px, 430px, and 600px: Workspace and Course bottom navigation remained available with direct Accueil, Mes parcours, Explorer, and Créer links.
- Authenticated Forge Learn response, source selection, Edit proposal Apply-to-local-draft, Resources, Publication, enrollment progress, and no-persist Save/Publish constraints were retained from F5.1 and rechecked in the F5.3 layouts.

## Remaining Divergences

- Real course covers are unavailable by contract, so the canonical placeholder intentionally replaces the photography/artwork in the design boards.
- Filter, sort, search, activity, profile, collaboration, and rating surfaces in the boards require data/contracts outside this sprint and are not represented as fake controls.
- Pixel overlays/diffs are intentionally not supplied because the available references are presentation boards rather than page-sized production baselines.
